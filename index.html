01 / History

The history of football.

Modern association football emerged through a long process of rule-making, organization, competition and international expansion.

Origins 1863 IFAB FIFA World Cup Global Era Modern Era
BEFORE MODERN ASSOCIATION FOOTBALL

Ball games existed long before modern football.

Different societies developed games involving balls, kicking, carrying, passing and competing for territory. These games varied greatly and should not simply be treated as identical versions of modern association football.

They are nevertheless part of the broader history of organized ball games from which later football traditions emerged.

MEDIEVAL & EARLY MODERN PERIOD

Folk football.

Various forms of folk football appeared in Britain during the medieval and early modern periods. Local communities often played according to customs rather than a universal written code.

These games could be very different from the organized 11-a-side game that developed centuries later.

1800s

Schools and clubs begin developing their own codes.

During the nineteenth century, football became increasingly organized in British schools and clubs. Different institutions used different rules concerning handling, kicking, physical contact and movement of the ball.

As teams increasingly wanted to play each other, the differences between local rules created a need for common laws.

26 OCTOBER 1863

The Football Association is founded.

The Football Association was formed in London on 26 October 1863. The organization worked toward creating a common code of football rules.

1863 ONWARD

The Laws of the Game evolve.

The laws developed through repeated amendments. Rules governing goals, free kicks, corners, penalties, offside, player conduct and many other aspects of football gradually changed over time.

The modern Laws of the Game are maintained internationally by The IFAB.

1886

The International Football Association Board.

The International Football Association Board was founded in 1886 by the four British football associations: England, Scotland, Wales and Ireland.

IFAB became the international guardian of the Laws of the Game. FIFA joined the board in 1913.

FOUNDED 1886
FOUNDERS 4 FAs
FIFA JOINS 1913
ROLE Laws of the Game
1888

League football.

The Football League was founded in England in 1888. Regular scheduled league competition became an important part of professional football's structure.

League systems subsequently became one of the defining organizational structures of club football around the world.

21 MAY 1904

FIFA is founded in Paris.

FIFA, the Fédération Internationale de Football Association, was founded in Paris on 21 May 1904.

The organization eventually became central to the administration of international association football and the organization of the FIFA World Cup.

YEAR 1904
PLACE Paris
ORGANIZATION FIFA
FOCUS International football
1913

FIFA joins IFAB.

FIFA joined IFAB in 1913, connecting the international football organization with the body responsible for the Laws of the Game.

1920s

The idea of a dedicated world championship.

International football continued expanding, creating pressure for a global championship separate from the Olympic football tournament.

FIFA approved the launch of the World Cup in 1928.

1930

The first FIFA World Cup.

Uruguay hosted the first FIFA World Cup in 1930. The hosts won the tournament, establishing the World Cup as a recurring global championship for men's national teams.

YEAR 1930
HOST Uruguay
WINNER Uruguay
TEAMS 13
1939–1945

Football during the Second World War.

The Second World War disrupted international football. The FIFA World Cup was not held in 1942 or 1946.

1950s–1980s

Football becomes increasingly global.

Postwar football expanded through national leagues, continental competitions, international tournaments and television broadcasting.

Football cultures developed across Europe, South America, Africa, Asia, North America and Oceania.

1955/56

The European Cup begins.

UEFA's European Champion Clubs' Cup began in 1955/56. Real Madrid won the inaugural competition.

The competition later became the UEFA Champions League, which was relaunched under that name in 1992/93.

1991

The first FIFA Women's World Cup.

FIFA organized the first Women's World Cup in 1991, creating a dedicated global championship for women's national teams.

1990s–2000s

Global broadcasting and commercialization.

Football became increasingly international through television, sponsorship, player transfers, global supporter communities and expanding professional competitions.

2000s–2010s

The data revolution.

Professional football increasingly adopted detailed statistical analysis, video analysis, player tracking, sports science and performance data.

2010s

Technology enters officiating.

Goal-line technology and later video review systems became part of the modern football environment.

2010s–2020s

VAR and the modern football era.

Video Assistant Referee technology became part of top-level football following trials and changes to the Laws of the Game.

Modern football now combines traditional competition with global broadcasting, analytics, technology, professional academies and digital media.
