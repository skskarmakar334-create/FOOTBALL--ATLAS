01 / History

The history of football.

A chronological journey through the development of association football.

Ancient Era

Early Ball Games

Various societies developed games involving kicking, carrying or controlling balls. These games were predecessors or cultural relatives of later football traditions.

Medieval Period

Folk Football

Different forms of community football developed in Britain and elsewhere. Rules varied greatly between communities and competitions.

19th Century

Rules Begin to Standardize

British schools and clubs began developing more formal rules, creating a need for standardized football regulations.

1863

The Laws of the Game

The Football Association was established in London and played a major role in standardizing association football's rules.

1880s–1900s

Professional & International Growth

Organized competitions expanded, professional football developed, and international matches became increasingly important.

1904

FIFA

FIFA was founded in Paris, creating an international governing organization for association football.

1930

First FIFA World Cup

Uruguay hosted the inaugural FIFA World Cup, establishing the tournament as a central event in international football history.

1950s–1990s

Global Expansion

Television, continental competitions, professional leagues and international stars expanded football's global audience.

2000s–Present

The Data & Technology Era

Advanced analytics, goal-line technology, VAR, global broadcasting and digital media transformed how football is played and followed.
